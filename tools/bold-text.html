Worth knowing: bold, italic and the other styles are separate Unicode characters, not real formatting. Screen readers often can't read them properly, and LinkedIn's search doesn't recognise them as normal keywords. Use them sparingly, for example for a headline or one key phrase. Letters from other alphabets such as Cyrillic have no styled versions and stay as they are.
