Job Search Launcher is now in your browser. Three quick steps and you’re searching every job site at once.

Extensions
Job Search Launcher
Click the pin
  1. Pin the icon. Click the puzzle icon in Chrome’s toolbar, top right, then the pin next to Job Search Launcher. The icon stays visible next to the address bar.
  2. Run your first search. Click the icon, type a job title and pick a country. Add a city, how recent the jobs should be and a work type if you like. Press Open, and every site opens in one tab group, newest jobs first.
  3. Save it for tomorrow. Press Save search and next time it’s one click. Your recent searches are kept too.
Good to know. Some sites can’t apply every filter from a link. The small label next to a site tells you which filter it will ignore. A site may ask you to confirm you are human the first time, that’s normal.
